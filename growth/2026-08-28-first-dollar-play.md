# LotVue360 First-Dollar Play

## Beginning artifact

Target buyer: independent used-car dealer with stale vehicle listings.

Problem: staff time and weak merchandising reduce lead conversion.

Free sample: produce one improved vehicle listing from dealer-provided public inventory data, clearly labeled as AI-assisted.

Paid order: $1,000 for a 10-vehicle merchandising sprint containing listing copy, image-order recommendations, calls to action, and a delivery report.

Retainer: $1,000 per month for up to 10 newly listed or aging vehicles, with a monthly before-and-after report.

Constraints: original clean-room work only; no copied dealer or competitor copy; no invented vehicle facts; no account creation, payment collection, or outbound publishing without owner-controlled access.

## Goal-post artifact

Finished means:

- One real dealer prospect is recorded with source evidence.
- One free sample is generated from verified public inventory facts.
- The sample passes factual, security, and IP review.
- A buyer-ready offer states scope, price, turnaround, exclusions, and AI involvement.
- Delivery templates exist for the 10-vehicle paid sprint.
- Outreach is sent through an authorized channel and logged.
- Buyer response is recorded.
- The first payment is confirmed by an authoritative payment record, not a projection.
- The completed order is delivered and acceptance is recorded.
- A retainer offer is presented after delivery.
