# LotVue360 Customer Onboarding — First-Dollar Kit

## Artifact 1: Beginning State

Customer has paid or entered an approved trial but has not activated a vehicle page.

Required intake:
- Dealer name and primary contact
- Inventory feed method
- Website domain
- Brand logo and colors
- First vehicle VIN
- Billing owner
- Support contact consent

Welcome message:

> Welcome to LotVue360. Your first goal is simple: publish one accurate, branded vehicle page. Send your dealership details and first VIN using the checklist below. We will use only the access required for setup.

Activation checklist:
1. Confirm dealer identity and authorized contact.
2. Validate the inventory source without altering dealer records.
3. Import one vehicle.
4. Review price, photos, features, and contact details.
5. Generate the vehicle page or approved embed.
6. Have the dealer approve the visible result.
7. Record activation time, issues, and next action.

## Seven-Day Onboarding Sequence

- Day 0: Welcome and secure intake.
- Day 1: First vehicle page review.
- Day 2: Inventory accuracy check.
- Day 3: Lead-routing test using test data.
- Day 5: Usage and merchandising guidance.
- Day 7: Activation review and expansion offer.

## Artifact 2: Finished Goalpost

Onboarding is complete only when:
- One real vehicle page is published or deployment-ready with dealer approval.
- Price, VIN, photos, dealership identity, and contact path are accurate.
- A test lead reaches the correct approved destination.
- Dealer staff can add or update a vehicle using documented steps.
- Billing status and renewal date are recorded.
- Security, consent, and support ownership are documented.
- The next revenue action is scheduled: paid activation, additional rooftop, or inventory expansion.

## Metrics

- Intake completion rate
- Time to first valid vehicle page
- Test-lead delivery success rate
- Seven-day activation rate
- Trial-to-paid conversion rate
- Additional-rooftop conversion rate
- Support issues per activated dealer

## Audit Record

Record the dealer, operator, date, input source, validation evidence, approval receipt, billing state, blockers, and next action. Never publish, charge, or modify external dealer systems without the authorized owner action required by the applicable workflow.
