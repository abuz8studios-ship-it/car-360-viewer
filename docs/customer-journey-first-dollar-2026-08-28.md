# LotVue360 First-Dollar Customer Journey

## Artifact 1 — Beginning Baseline

Buyer: Independent dealership owner or general manager.
Pain: Vehicle listings are incomplete, inconsistent, or fail to convert shoppers into leads.
Trigger: The dealer sees a weak listing, aging inventory, or low lead volume.
Free sample: Audit one live vehicle detail page and deliver three specific conversion fixes.
Call to action: Approve a fixed-scope $1,000 merchandising sprint for selected inventory.
Paid deliverable: Correct listing content, improve buyer-facing presentation, verify public pages, and provide before/after evidence.
Retainer transition: Offer ongoing inventory QA, merchandising updates, and conversion reporting.
Primary friction: Dealer distrust, unclear proof, integration risk, and fear of recurring software overhead.
Required proof: One real VIN, visible before/after changes, working lead path, delivery timeline, and fixed price.

## Artifact 2 — Finished Goalpost

The journey is finished when one identified dealership has received a free one-VIN audit, accepted a $1,000 paid sprint, received verified production-grade deliverables, and received a documented monthly-retainer offer. Evidence must include the dealer identifier, audited VIN and URL, dated sample, paid scope, delivery receipt, verification results, buyer response, and next follow-up date. Payments, signatures, account creation, or financial commitments remain owner-gated.

## Journey Stages

| Stage | Buyer question | ABUZ8 action | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Why are my listings underperforming? | Show one concrete listing defect and its buyer impact. | Dealer acknowledges the issue. |
| Trust | Can ABUZ8 prove it? | Deliver a free one-VIN audit with three prioritized fixes. | Audit delivered and viewed. |
| Purchase | What exactly do I get for $1,000? | Present a fixed-scope merchandising sprint with deadline and acceptance criteria. | Buyer accepts scope; Ahmad handles payment or signature. |
| Delivery | Did the work improve the listing? | Ship changes and verify the public page and lead path. | Before/after evidence and test receipt. |
| Retainer | Who keeps inventory clean? | Offer monthly QA, merchandising, and reporting tied to dealer workload replaced. | Retainer offer delivered and follow-up scheduled. |
