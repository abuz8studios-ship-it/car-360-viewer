# LotVue360 UI/UX Sales Readiness

## Beginning Artifact — Current Baseline

Status: Unverified until the live product, source, mobile layout, and payment path are inspected.

Audit these revenue-critical surfaces:

- Landing-page promise and primary CTA
- Dealer pain, outcome, and proof above the fold
- Product screenshots or working demo
- Pricing and plan comparison
- Dealer onboarding form
- Payment and confirmation flow
- Mobile usability and accessibility
- Trust, privacy, security, and support language
- Analytics events from visit through purchase

Do not mark an item complete from source code alone. Record a visible receipt, tested URL, or successful transaction-path result.

## Goalpost Artifact — Finished Means

LotVue360 is UI/UX sale-ready only when:

1. A dealer understands the product, audience, outcome, and next action within five seconds.
2. One primary CTA is visually dominant and works on desktop and mobile.
3. Pricing clearly states inclusions, billing cadence, cancellation terms, and any setup fee.
4. Real product visuals demonstrate the VDP, inventory, merchandising, and lead experience.
5. The onboarding form validates input, prevents duplicate submission, and shows a clear success state.
6. The payment path completes successfully in the intended environment and produces a customer receipt.
7. Empty, loading, validation, payment-failure, and server-error states provide recovery actions.
8. Keyboard navigation, focus states, labels, contrast, and touch targets pass an accessibility review.
9. Privacy, terms, security, company identity, contact, and support links are visible and functional.
10. Analytics capture landing_view, primary_cta_click, pricing_view, checkout_start, checkout_success, and checkout_failure.
11. A mobile viewport test and a real browser test show no clipped content, blocked controls, or horizontal overflow.
12. Evidence is recorded with URLs, screenshots, timestamps, test results, and remaining owner-only blockers.

## Revenue Conversion Rule

Every visual element must support one of four jobs: explain value, establish trust, demonstrate the product, or advance the buyer. Remove or demote anything that does none of these.
