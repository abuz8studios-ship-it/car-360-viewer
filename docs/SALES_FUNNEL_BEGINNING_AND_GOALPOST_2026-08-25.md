# LotVue360 Sales Funnel

Date: 2026-08-25
Owner: ABUZ8 LLC
Department: ASRED — Pillar 3

## Beginning artifact

LotVue360 exists as an automotive merchandising product. Before public promotion, verify the current build, live URL, dealer provisioning flow, vehicle-detail-page behavior, pricing authority, payment status, security checks, and customer-facing claims. Unverified capabilities must not appear as facts in sales copy.

## Offer hypothesis

Buyer: independent dealerships that need faster, more consistent vehicle merchandising.

Problem: preparing and maintaining useful online vehicle presentations consumes dealership time and can produce inconsistent listings.

Promise: help dealerships turn inventory data into consistent, customer-ready vehicle presentations through one managed workflow.

Primary CTA: Request a live inventory demo.

Secondary CTA: Start a dealership readiness review.

## Funnel path

1. Targeted dealership outreach or industry content.
2. Landing page focused on merchandising consistency and saved staff effort.
3. Demo request form capturing dealership, inventory size, current provider, contact details, and main bottleneck.
4. Fifteen-minute qualification and live inventory demonstration.
5. Written pilot offer with verified scope, price, onboarding requirements, and support terms.
6. Authorized payment link or invoice.
7. Dealer provisioning, acceptance check, and testimonial request after demonstrated value.

## Landing-page copy skeleton

Headline: Turn dealership inventory into consistent, customer-ready vehicle presentations.

Subheadline: LotVue360 gives automotive teams a focused workflow for preparing and presenting inventory without adding another manual merchandising burden.

CTA: Request a Live Inventory Demo

Trust section: Show only verified screenshots, supported integrations, measured results, security statements, and customer evidence.

## Proof required before launch

- Confirm the canonical live product and deployment state.
- Confirm the demo-request destination and owner notification path.
- Confirm authorized pricing and billing cadence.
- Complete a real dealer provisioning test.
- Test the public vehicle-detail experience on desktop and mobile.
- Verify input escaping, tenant separation, authentication, and recovery behavior.
- Capture truthful screenshots and one end-to-end demo receipt.
- Remove or qualify every claim that lacks current evidence.

## Goalpost artifact — finished means

- One verified landing page is live on the approved domain.
- The CTA submits successfully and creates a retrievable lead receipt.
- The demo flow works with representative dealership inventory.
- Pricing, scope, cancellation terms, and support boundaries are approved and visible.
- The authorized payment flow completes successfully in the intended mode.
- A dealership can be provisioned and reach an accepted vehicle-detail page.
- Analytics record landing-page visit, CTA, qualified lead, proposal, checkout, and paid activation.
- Security and mobile checks pass with stored evidence.
- The first qualified dealership receives a traceable path from visit to paid activation.
