Free sample: Produce one branded vehicle presentation from dealer-provided inventory data. Obtain permission before using customer assets. Do not request passwords by email.

Baseline checklist:
- Confirm buyer, dealership, website, inventory source, and desired outcome.
- Record current merchandising workflow and human alternative.
- Define one sample vehicle and written acceptance criteria.
- Confirm approved asset access and data handling boundaries.
- State honestly that AI assists production when asked.
- Quote the paid order based on replaced human work and buyer value.

## Revenue path

1. Free sample: one vehicle presentation with a clear before-and-after result.
2. Paid order: scoped dealership setup with price, delivery date, and acceptance criteria.
3. Retainer: recurring inventory merchandising, monitoring, and monthly reporting.

Payments, signatures, account creation, live Stripe changes, and commitments over $1,000 require Ahmad.

## Goal-post artifact — finished means

- Customer approved the sample in writing.
- Paid scope, price, owner, and delivery date are documented.
- Required assets were received through an approved channel.
- Dealer configuration is isolated and contains no exposed secrets.
- Public vehicle page passes mobile, desktop, link, image, and input-safety checks.
- Customer can complete the primary workflow without ABUZ8 assistance.
- Acceptance evidence and outstanding issues are stored with exact paths and timestamps.
- Payment status is verified, never assumed.
- Retainer offer is delivered after acceptance.
- Onboarding is closed only when access, support contact, rollback path, and next review date are documented.
