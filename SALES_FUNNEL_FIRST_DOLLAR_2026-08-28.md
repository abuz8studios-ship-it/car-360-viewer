# LotVue360 First-Dollar Sales Funnel

## Beginning artifact

- Product: LotVue360 vehicle merchandising and interactive inventory presentation.
- Buyer: Independent dealership owner or general manager with 40–250 vehicles.
- Pain: Inventory pages are incomplete, visually inconsistent, and expensive to update manually.
- Human alternative: Staff or contractors repeatedly photograph, prepare, and publish vehicle merchandising.
- Current gate: No buyer is counted without a recorded response, order, or payment receipt.

## Funnel

1. Free sample: Produce one dealership-branded vehicle presentation using one public inventory vehicle. Deliver a before/after link or image and three specific merchandising findings.
2. Paid order: $1,000 fixed-price launch covering dealer branding, inventory presentation setup, deployment, and a verified live example.
3. Retainer: $500 per month for inventory monitoring, presentation updates, issue correction, and a monthly merchandising report.

## Message

Subject: I built a free vehicle-page upgrade for [Dealer]

I reviewed one vehicle on your website and prepared a dealership-branded presentation showing how the same inventory can look clearer and more complete. I will send the sample free. If you want it applied to your inventory, the fixed launch is $1,000. Ongoing monitoring and updates are $500 per month. No contract is required for the sample.

CTA: Reply SAMPLE with the dealership website and the vehicle you want reviewed.

## Qualification

Proceed when the dealer has at least 40 active vehicles, a public inventory feed or pages, and an owner or manager who can approve merchandising. Do not promise unsupported integrations, traffic growth, or sales results.

## Tracking

Record dealer name, website, inventory count, decision-maker, sample vehicle, sample-delivery date, response, paid-order status, and retainer status. A projection is not revenue.

## Finished goalpost artifact

- One real dealer selected and recorded.
- One free sample produced and verified.
- Sample delivered to a named decision-maker.
- Response logged.
- $1,000 paid deployment offered in writing.
- First dollar confirmed by payment evidence before reporting revenue.
- Retainer offered only after the paid deployment is delivered and verified.
- All claims remain truthful about AI assistance, capabilities, and results.
