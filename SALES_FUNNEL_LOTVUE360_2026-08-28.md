Entry offer: Free teardown of one live vehicle detail page.
Proof delivered: Five prioritized findings covering mobile presentation, buyer trust, calls to action, merchandising, and lead friction.
Compliance: Use only observable evidence. Make no unsupported revenue or conversion claims. Disclose AI assistance when asked.

Proceed when the dealer has active inventory, a public website, a named decision-maker, and authority to approve a $1,000 project. Escalate payments, signatures, account creation, and financial commitments to Ahmad.

## Goalpost Artifact — Finished Means

1. One named dealership and decision-maker are recorded in CRM.
2. One teardown is produced from the dealer's live VDP using verified observations.
3. The teardown includes a direct $1,000 launch-sprint offer.
4. Follow-up offers the $1,000 monthly optimization retainer after delivery.
5. Every outreach, reply, order, and revenue event is logged.
6. No unsupported claims, copied assets, secrets, or unapproved financial actions appear in the funnel.
7. The loop is closed with one of three statuses: paid order, explicit rejection, or dated follow-up.
